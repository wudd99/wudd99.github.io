Yihao Wu – PhD Student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at UCSB

Welcome!

Greetings from Yihao! I am currently a 3rd-year PhD student in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) at the University of California, Santa Barbara (UCSB). I am honored to have my PhD co-advised by Prof. Luke Theogarajan from UCSB, and Prof. Bongjin Kim from KAIST (Daejeon, Republic of Korea). Before joining UCSB, I earned a Bachelor of Engineering in Electronic Science and Technology and a Bachelor of Management in Information Management and Information Systems from Beijing University of Technology, as well as a Master of Science in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida.

My research interests include digital and mixed-signal in-memory computing, and ASIC-based accelerators for Combinatorial Optimization Problems (COPs) and Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Outside of academia, I enjoy playing badminton, swimming, photography, and gaming. One of my dreams is to own a pickup truck (Especially Ford F-150 Raptor) and travel the world with a cat and a capybara by my side.
